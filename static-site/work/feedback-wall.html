FEEDBACK WALL: Where Every Insight Becomes Part of the Big Picture

A real-time visualization tool transforming attendee insights into a dynamic digital mosaic.

Project Overview

HighBeam developed an interactive Feedback Wall for a major cybersecurity forum. The goal was simple. Help people feel like their ideas mattered and show the conversations of the room coming to life in real time.

The Experience

We built a two screen setup. A 21 inch touchscreen kiosk where attendees could draw or write their thoughts, and a large LED wall that instantly turned every submission into a living digital mosaic. People could see their input appear right in front of them and become part of something bigger. The installation made the forum feel more connected. You could feel the crowd pause, look up at the wall, and recognize that their individual voice contributed to a shared picture of the event.

What We Delivered

An inviting input experience

A guided, secure flow that encouraged attendees to take part without hesitation.

A live visual mosaic

A responsive display that transformed every insight into motion and color in real time.

A stronger sense of community

A centerpiece that made participants feel heard and helped the event feel more collaborative and human.
