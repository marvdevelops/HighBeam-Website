DFI GOLDEN VOICE STUDIO: Step In, Speak Up, Star in Your Own Scene

An interactive recording studio experience celebrating the art of voice acting and dubbing.

Project Overview

HighBeam developed the Golden Voice Studio to give visitors a chance to experience the fun of dubbing their own movie scene. It was designed to be playful, expressive, and memorable.

The Experience

Guests stepped up to the kiosk, chose a movie clip, then entered a small recording booth where they performed their voiceover. The system processed everything instantly and played back their newly dubbed scene. You could hear people laugh, redo their lines, and call their friends over to watch. It became one of those installations people talk about. Not because of the technology alone, but because it made them feel creative and confident enough to try something new.

What We Delivered

Friendly bilingual interface

An easy to use design that made both Arabic and English speaking visitors feel comfortable.

Smooth real time processing

Audio and video handling that stayed responsive during constant use.

A takeaway people were proud of

Personalized dubbed clips that participants saved, shared, and remembered.
